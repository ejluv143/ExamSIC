# Team Relay: teacher guide

In a Team Relay, small teams race to open a secret password. Each member answers their own share of the quiz; every right answer reveals one piece of the password. The team puts the pieces together and submits the password.

## Before you start

- Use a quiz with no essay or drawing questions. It needs at least as many questions as the largest team has members (at least 2 to 4).
- Start a session, choose **Game**, set pacing to **Student-paced**, and switch on **Team Relay**. Teacher-paced games can't be relays.

## Set it up

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Team size | Most members per team (2 to 4). |
| Number of teams | Teams made when the lobby opens (1 to 50). You can add or remove teams in the lobby. |
| Let students pick their team | Lets students join or switch teams in the lobby. |
| Piece kind | What one right answer reveals: letters, words, numbered letters (coordinates), or your clues. |
| Password | Your own password, or leave it empty and each team gets a generated word. Clue pieces need your password and at least one clue. |
| Password guesses | Wrong guesses a team may make (1 to 20). Each wrong guess also costs 100 points. |
| Solve bonus | Points for solving; the full bonus at the start, half at the end of the round. |
| Round minutes | Minutes from the start (1 to 120). |
| Require consensus | A guess is only checked after every member confirms it. |
| Allow retry | After a wrong answer a member may try the same question again (1 to 3 times), losing a percentage of the points each time. |
| Allow teammates to rescue questions | A member who finished their own questions may answer a teammate's locked question once, for a percentage of its points. |

## Form teams in the lobby

1. Open the lobby from the presenter screen. Students join with the join key and start unassigned.
2. Students pick a team if you allowed it. You can turn picking off at any time with **Allow students to pick their team**.
3. Use the lobby tools:
   - **Move a player**: drag them to a team, or use the menu under their name (also back to Unassigned). This overrides their pick.
   - **Randomize teams**: deals everyone into the teams at random.
   - **Balance teams**: evens out sizes with the fewest moves; unassigned players fill the smallest teams.
   - **Add team / Remove team**: a removed team's members become unassigned. The last team can't be removed.
4. Press **Start the game**. Unassigned players are balanced into teams and empty teams are dropped. The game won't start if a team has only one player or more than the size limit; the message names the team.

Teams can't be changed once the game has started.

## During the game

- The presenter screen shows each team's progress (pieces unlocked, wrong guesses, points). Passwords and pieces stay hidden until a team finishes.
- Students see right or wrong at once, but not the correct answer.
- **Retry on:** a wrong answer can be retried only while the member is still on that question and has retries left.
- **Rescue on:** when a teammate's question is locked, a member who finished their own questions can claim it. Only one member can claim it, and they get one try.
- A player who joins late takes over questions their teammates haven't seen yet. If a player is removed, their unanswered questions go to teammates who aren't finished; with none left, they stay open for rescue (if on).
- A team is finished when it solves the password, runs out of guesses, or the round time ends. The game ends when every team is finished.

## Run the debrief

When the game ends, the presenter shows every team's debrief: the password, each question with the correct answer, and what the team answered. Use it to:

1. Rank the teams: solved first (fastest), then by points.
2. Go through the **most missed questions** shown in the standings, and explain the correct answers.
3. Ask teams which piece was the hardest to unlock and how they worked together.

Standings, including each player's team and rescues, are on the session results page and download as Excel. Grading works as in any game: speed and team bonuses affect game points only, not the normal score. Each student's score counts the whole quiz, so questions that went to teammates count as unanswered; consider turning off **Count in the class record** for relay sessions.
