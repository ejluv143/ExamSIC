# Files

- [Exam mode and anti-cheating](exam-mode-and-integrity.md) - Per-session anti-cheat settings, what the browser detects and blocks, what the API enforces (device binding, IP allowlist, room password, late join, computers-only, pledge), exam-mode locked settings and device-switch approval, and how integrity levels and cross-student comparisons are computed.
- [Game mode](game-mode.md) - Kahoot-style teacher-paced and Wayground-style student-paced games — the server-side Game engine with in-memory rooms rebuilt from PostgreSQL, the 500 ms ticker, speed and streak points, teacher clock controls, joining with keys (including guests), kicking players, and the per-viewer GameView streamed over WebSocket.
- [Live sessions over WebSocket](live-sessions.md) - How teachers watch sessions and students receive pauses, locks, deadlines and warnings in real time — single-use HMAC tickets, the LiveRpcs WebSocket streams, the in-memory LiveHub over PostgreSQL state, and the teacher's live controls.
- [Mastery mode](mastery-mode.md) - Self-paced practice sessions where each answer is graded at once and missed questions return later in a per-attempt queue until mastered or out of tries; covers the queue rules, settings, feedback and explanations, and the masteryState/masteryAnswer RPCs.
